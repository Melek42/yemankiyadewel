About Mehreteab Asefa

ስለ ምህረተአብ አሰፋ

Memeher Mehreteab Asefa

Founder & Teacher at Ye mankiya dewol

Memeher Mehreteab Asefa is dedicated to promoting Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahdo Church values globally. Through "Ye mankiya dewol" (የማንቂያ ደወል), he provides essential information to bridge the communication gap for Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahdo Church followers worldwide.

With over 565 videos and a growing community of 474,000+ subscribers, his teachings reach Ethiopians across the globe, helping them stay connected to their faith, heritage, and traditions.

Our Mission

To teach God's Word, preserve Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahdo Church values, and share Ethiopia's rich religious heritage, history, and tradition with Ethiopians everywhere.

Our Vision

To create a globally connected Ethiopian Orthodox community, deeply rooted in faith and aware of their rich spiritual heritage.
