Memeher Mehreteab Asefa • መምህር ምህረተአብ አሰፋ

Memeher Mehreteab Asefa

መምህር ምህረተአብ አሰፋ

Memeher Mehreteab Asefa is a dedicated teacher and promoter of Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahdo Church values. Through his channel "Ye mankiya dewol" (የማንቂያ ደወል), he has been serving the global Ethiopian Orthodox community for over 15 years.

His work focuses on teaching God's Word, preserving Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahdo Church traditions, and sharing Ethiopia's rich religious heritage with Ethiopians worldwide. His daily and weekly programs bridge the communication gap for followers around the globe.

"የማንቂያ ደወል ማለት ለህዝበ ክርስቲያን የሚደረግ አገልግሎት ሲሆን ትክክለኛውን የሃይማኖት ትምህርት ለማድረስ ያግዛል"

— Mehreteab Asefa

With over 474,000 subscribers and 565+ videos, his channel has become a vital resource for Ethiopians seeking authentic religious education and cultural connection, no matter where they are in the world.

Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

Our Mission
