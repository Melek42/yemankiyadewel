Our Purpose

ማንቂያ ደወል
The Alarm Bell

"Ye mankiya dewol" (የማንቂያ ደወል) serves as a spiritual alarm bell, awakening and guiding the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahdo community worldwide. Through the dedicated work of Memeher Mehreteab Asefa, we provide daily and weekly programs that bridge the communication gap for followers around the globe.

Our Mission

አላማችን

To teach God's Word, preserve Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahdo Church values, and share Ethiopia's rich religious heritage, history, and tradition with Ethiopians everywhere.

Through daily and weekly programs, we bridge the communication gap for Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahdo Church followers around the world.
